Masternode stake operations on XDC

Vote stake onto a candidate to move it from standby into the masternode set, unvote when you need to, and withdraw once the unlock period ends. Every action goes straight to the XDC validator contract, signed in MetaMask or prepared for your custody platform.

Things to know

It talks to the system contract only

Every read and transaction goes to the XDC validator contract at xdc0000000000000000000000000000000000000088, the same contract the official XDC Master dashboard uses. There’s no intermediary contract and nothing is held by XDC Outpost.

Custody platforms and multisig

Watch your staking address, then use “Prepare unsigned transaction” to get the exact destination, value and call data. Paste them into your custody platform’s contract-call screen or a multisig proposal. The withdrawal must be signed by the same address that unvoted.

The unlock period

Unvoted stake can be withdrawn after the voter unlock period. The block it unlocks at is fixed when you unvote, and the timing shown is an estimate from recent block times.

Owner minimum stake

The address that owns a node must keep at least the owner minimum staked on it, so unvote can’t go below that. Recovering the owner stake means resigning the node, which takes it out of the candidate list.

Rank is by stake, not a guarantee

XDC’s consensus allows up to 108 masternodes, chosen by stake each epoch, and skips nodes penalised for missing blocks. When your RPC shares the live masternode list, the status shown is the real one for the current epoch. Otherwise it’s estimated from stake. Other candidates’ stakes move too, so leave a margin.

Your choice of RPC

Reads use the free public Plugin XDC RPC by default. Under Network settings you can switch to your own node or a private endpoint. It’s saved only in this browser.
