Things to know

Your XDC goes straight to the network

Votes, unvotes and withdrawals are made directly with XDC’s built-in staking contract (xdc…0088), the same one the official XDC masternode dashboard uses. XDC Outpost never holds your funds or your keys.

Signing with a custody platform or multisig

Enter your staking address in step 1, then choose “Prepare unsigned transaction”. You get the destination, amount and call data to paste into your platform’s contract-call screen or a multisig proposal. Withdrawals must be signed by the same address that unvoted.

The unlock period

Unvoted stake can be withdrawn after the unlock period. The unlock block is set when you unvote. Times shown are estimates based on recent block speed.

If you own the node

The node’s owner must keep at least the owner minimum staked on it, so you can’t unvote below that. Getting it back means resigning the node, which removes it from the candidate list. Stake from a resigned node can be withdrawn after the resignation period, longer than the unvote period.

Masternode status isn’t guaranteed

Each epoch, up to 108 nodes with the most stake become masternodes. Nodes penalised for missing blocks sit out until the penalty clears. Other people’s stakes change all the time, so if you’re voting to get a node in, add a margin.

Check before you sign

Each transaction is checked against the network before MetaMask opens, so one that would fail is stopped with an explanation. Always confirm the node address and amount in MetaMask or your custody platform.

Try it on the testnet first

Switch to the Apothem testnet under “Network and connection” to practise voting, unvoting and withdrawing with free test XDC (TXDC) before using real funds. Get TXDC from the Apothem faucet.

Your connection

The page reads the network through the free public Plugin XDC RPC. If you run your own node or have a private endpoint, you can use it instead. Your choice is remembered in this browser only.
